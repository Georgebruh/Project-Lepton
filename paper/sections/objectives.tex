\section{Objectives}\label{sec:objectives}

This study keeps the four objectives of the project proposal, worded here as they were tested.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item To forecast next week's number of leptospirosis cases in QC for hospitals and health
    offices, as the expected cases, a 90\% range and the probability of crossing the epidemic
    threshold.
  \item To estimate, from rain forecast one to three days before a storm, the prophylaxis stock
    that each barangay and health center needs under DM~\mbox{2023-0309}, as stock-planning
    figures, not prescriptions; and to run this on archived forecasts and on observed rain for
    four past storms, comparing the barangays it flags with those that reports named as flooded.
  \item To size every model's forecast range from its own past misses, so that its 90\% range
    holds the observed count in about 90\% of weeks.
  \item To test whether the proposed negative binomial regression, M4, forecasts more accurately
    than the published Philippine benchmark, M3, a Prophet--XGBoost hybrid
    \parencite{latigay2026} that is first reproduced on its authors' own data. In a weekly
    rolling-origin backtest, the primary endpoint is the difference between M4's and M3's mean
    absolute errors, with a 95\% moving-block bootstrap interval; the weighted interval score and
    the Brier and Brier skill scores are secondary measures.
\end{enumerate}
Section~\ref{sec:results-layer1} reports on objectives 1, 3 and 4, and
Section~\ref{sec:results-layer2} on objective 2.
