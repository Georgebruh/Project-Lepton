Typhoon Carina (Jul 2024) [`carina_2024`] : Storm 1
Typhoon Carina (Gaemi) enhanced the Southwest Monsoon (Habagat) in late July 2024, dumping record 24-hour rainfall over Quezon City (345.0 mm at PAGASA Science Garden) and triggering citywide river and street inundation.

Day flooding began : 2024-07-23

Days flooded: 3 days and 2024-07-23 to 2024-07-25 (Sensitivity bounds D ± 1: 2, 3, 4 days)

July–August 2025 Floods [`floods_2025`] : Storm 2
Successive tropical cyclones (STS Crising/Wipha, Dante, and Emong) enhanced the Southwest Monsoon from July 19 to 24, 2025, placing Quezon City under a State of Calamity and triggering the early-August 2025 QCESD leptospirosis outbreak.

August 2026 Habagat Floods [`habagat_2026`] : Storm 3
Intensified Southwest Monsoon surges in August 2026 (peaking August 8–11, with secondary surges on August 17 and August 29–30 when La Mesa Dam reached spilling level) caused up to head-deep flooding and forced evacuations across low-lying Quezon City barangays.
